Daikatana's game code, in FTE.

From the Daikatana restoration project (dlls/physics, dlls/world, dlls/weapons, user = IonCommon; the headers
they take from the engine in include/). The game was three DLLs; here they're one: physics' loader
(physics/dk_static.cpp) calls world's and weapons' dll_Entry itself and looks their exports up in
fte/dk_exports.cpp, and what more than one of them defined is renamed in physics and weapons
(include/dk_rename_*.h). Both of those are generated by mkstatic.py from the objects' symbols (nm -A).

Building: sources.txt lists what's compiled, each with -include include/dk_fte_compat.h (what MSVC and its CRT
had, elsewhere), -DDK_MODULE_<directory> (physics, world, weapons, user, fte), -DTONGUE_ENGLISH -DC_ONLY, the
directories user, include, physics, world, weapons and the source's own on the include path. C++ (gnu++11,
-fpermissive: it was written for MSVC 6).

Changes from the original are marked FTE where they aren't mechanical: const CVector& for what MSVC let
temporaries bind to, MSVC 6's for-scope (loop variables hoisted), Windows-only bits.
